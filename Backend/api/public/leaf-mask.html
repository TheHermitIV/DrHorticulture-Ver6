01 / ADD PHOTOS

Start with one plant

Choose Add photos. You can select several images at once, then pick one from the photo queue.

Center one plant, keep the visible leaves in frame, and use even light. The left canvas shows the original and the right canvas is editable. The practice plant lets you try the tools without adding anything to the dataset.

02 / EDIT

Paint and refine

Paint / Erase
Drag on the right canvas to add foliage or remove background.
Brush / Zoom
Choose a smaller brush for edges and thin fronds. Use Fit to see the whole photo or zoom in for detail.
Show mask
Hide or show the green overlay to compare your selection with the photo.
Undo / Clear
Undo reverses the last mask edit. Clear empties the mask so you can start over.
03 / SUGGEST

Get a starting mask

Box
Drag a rectangle around the target foliage on the right canvas.
+ Leaf / − Background
Tap representative leaf pixels and unwanted pixels, such as soil or neighboring leaves.
Generate suggestion
Use a box or at least one leaf point. The suggestion replaces the current mask unless Add to mask is checked. Then refine it with Paint and Erase.
Clear prompts
Removes the box and points while keeping the current mask.

Suggestions use the colors in your photo. Check the mask before accepting it.

04 / REVIEW

Check every boundary

Include visible leaves of the target plant, even yellow or shaded ones. Leave out pots, soil, background, and neighboring plants.

Enter a reviewer name and confirm all three checks. Notes and a paired GreenSeeker NDVI reading (0–0.99) are optional. Leave NDVI blank when you don’t have a reading.

Save draft keeps unfinished work in this browser. Accept reviewed mask marks a completed mask for export. After changing an accepted mask, review and accept it again before exporting.

05 / DOWNLOAD

Take your results with you

Download mask PNG saves the current black-and-white mask. Download dataset exports only accepted entries in a ZIP with the JPEGs, masks, review records, and training manifest.

Save or accept edits before switching photos. Downloads are how you move work to another device or share it with the training team. Clearing this browser’s site data removes its saved workspace.
